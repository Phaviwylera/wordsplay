Pasted phrases stay only in this tab’s memory for export until you reload, leave, or remove the wallet. They are not sent to balance providers.

Bitcoin (BTC), TRON (TRX), and BNB Smart Chain (BNB) show native balances only, without USD prices. Tokens, NFTs and Bitcoin inscriptions are not scanned. Bitcoin is one address, not the entire wallet. If an Ethereum, Optimism or Arbitrum indexer fails, an RPC may return the ETH balance only; missing token and NFT data remains an incomplete check.
Security noteA seed phrase gives full control of your funds. Never share it; enter it only on a site and device you trust. This app is experimental and unaudited; do not use phrases protecting valuable funds. Pasted phrases are processed locally; only public addresses are sent to balance providers. A selected .txt file is sent to EC2 through a private SSH tunnel and processed there. EC2 temporarily retains encrypted phrases while scanning and keeps matches with detected balances; the encryption key is stored on the same EC2 volume. Administrators, a compromised server, snapshots or backups may expose phrases; deleting a job is not guaranteed secure erasure. Files derive only the first EVM account (m/44'/60'/0'/0/0), not Bitcoin or TRON addresses.
